# Research Roadmap — Project Atlas

Future research directions and prioritized investigations.

## Cycle 1 (Current) — Broad Discovery

| Priority | Domain | Key Questions | Status |
|----------|--------|---------------|--------|
| high | fundamentals | What is Atlas? What concepts, terminology, prerequisites apply? | pending |
| high | technology-landscape | Which languages, frameworks, platforms, tools could support Atlas? | pending |
| high | architecture | What system architectures, patterns, deployment approaches are viable? | pending |
| high | real-world-applications | What industry problems could Atlas address? Who are the users? | pending |
| medium | academic-literature | What research papers, standards, and academic projects are relevant? | pending |
| medium | innovation | What unconventional approaches or combinations are overlooked? | pending |
| high | critical-analysis | What are the limitations, security issues, costs, failure points? | pending |

## Cycle 2 (Planned) — Deep Dives

To be determined after cycle 1 synthesis. Likely candidates:

- Deep dive into the most promising architecture pattern(s).
- Feasibility study of top candidate technologies.
- Experiment proposals for highest-uncertainty, highest-impact areas.

## Cycle 3+ — Validation & Direction

- Prototype experiment execution.
- Architecture commitment decision.
- Go / no-go on implementation.

## Prioritization Criteria

- Importance to Atlas objective.
- Relevance to possible directions.
- Uncertainty (how little is known).
- Potential impact if validated.
- Research effort required.

High-value investigations (high importance + high uncertainty + manageable effort) are prioritized.
